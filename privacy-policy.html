MovieTB is a news and entertainment aggregator app. We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

MovieTB follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they use the app. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. MovieTB has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. MovieTB does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.
